HIGH — Next release, keep all Free for growth
1. Connection Quality Score — Streaming / Gaming / Calls verdicts
Turn numbers into answers: "Great for 4K streaming, Poor for gaming, Good for Zoom." Cloudflare's AIM does exactly this — it assigns points from "Latency, Packet Loss, Download, Upload, Loaded Latency, Jitter" and translates them to streaming/gaming/RTC scores. Effort: S (rules on data you already collect, + packet loss from #2). This is your headline screenshot for the Store.

2. Loaded Latency + Packet Loss + Bufferbloat Grade (A–F)
Ping while downloading/uploading, not just idle. Cloudflare reports "download, upload, latency, jitter, packet loss, and loaded vs. unloaded latency" — that difference is bufferbloat, the #1 reason "100 Mbps but Zoom lags." Effort: M. Huge differentiator vs basic Ookla-style apps.

3. Live Test Graph + Ping at 3 stages
Real-time download/upload curve during the test, plus ping idle / download / upload — Ookla: "Measure ping at 3 stages: idle, download, and upload". Your big empty dashboard area is perfect for this. Effort: M.

4. Server Selection + ISP Info
Auto-nearest server, manual picker, favourite server, and on the dashboard/result: ISP name, server city + distance. Effort: M (depends on your test backend having >1 endpoint — if it doesn't, show ISP + single server location first).

5. Video Streaming Test
Separate test: max resolution (SD/HD/4K/8K), load time, buffering %. Ookla's version is to "Take a video test to measure your max resolution, load time, and buffering". Users understand this better than Mbps. Effort: M.

6. Shareable Result Card
One click → branded orange PNG card (Download/Upload/Ping, Quality Score, ISP, date) to copy/save/share. This is your free viral loop — every share is an ad for Test Net Speed. Effort: S.

MEDIUM — Release after that, still Free
