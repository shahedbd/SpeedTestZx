# Speed Test ZX: App Description, Features and Pro vs Free

## Short description

**Speed Test ZX** is a fast, clean internet speed test for Windows. Measure download, upload, ping and jitter in seconds, and get plain answers on whether your connection is good enough for gaming, streaming and video calls. Every result is saved on your PC. The ISP Report Card shows whether you're getting the speed you pay for, and the Connection Monitor tracks when your internet slows down or drops.

**One-liner (Store tagline):** Test your internet speed, anytime, and prove whether you're getting what you pay for.

---

## Core features

### Speed testing
- **Real-time speed test**: download, upload, ping and jitter on an animated speedometer.
- **Quality verdicts**: plain-English ratings for **Gaming**, **Streaming** and **Video calls**, each with the reason behind it (for example, "latency jumps to 180 ms under load").
- **Bufferbloat grade (A–F)**: how much your latency rises when the line is busy.
- **Streaming estimate**: the resolution your connection can handle and your buffering risk.

### Dashboard
- Your last test result and live internet status at a glance.
- **Live traffic graph**: this PC's download and upload over the last 60 seconds, plus a latency check every 2 seconds.
- Recent-tests summary (last 10 tests) with average, best and worst.

### Test history
- Every result is saved on your PC and you can browse it anytime, newest first.
- You can clear your history whenever you want. Nothing is uploaded.

### Network info and tools
- **Network Info**: details about your network adapter and connection.
- **Network Tools**: built-in **Ping**, **DNS Lookup**, **IP Lookup** and **Traceroute**.

### ISP Report Card
- Enter your ISP plan once, and every test shows the **% of plan** you actually got.
- Delivered vs promised speed summary, with a **PDF complaint report** you can send to your ISP (Pro).

### Connection Monitor
- **Automatic background tests** on a schedule.
- **Outage detection and log**, plus latency and packet-loss tracking.
- **Alerts** when your internet goes down (and smart alerts in Pro).

### App experience
- **Dark and Light** themes, DPI-aware from 100% to 200%.
- Collapsible sidebar navigation.
- **System tray** support with notifications. You can minimize to the tray and start with Windows.
- Searchable settings that save instantly.
